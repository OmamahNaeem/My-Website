## 1. Introduction

We are three students — Omamah Naeem, Muhammad Wasif Ul Zaman, and Muhammad Ahmad Shah — who built this website as part of our AICT academic project. Our goal was to create an online book store called **Page Flippers** that allows users to browse and purchase books across three categories: Urdu Novels, Islamic Holy Books, and Competitive Exam Books.

We used Wix, a free website builder, to design and develop the entire website from scratch. The website is live and accessible at:

🌐 **https://wasifzaman2047.wixsite.com/mysite**

Since we used the free version of Wix, some features like online payment, custom domain, and removal of Wix branding were not available to us. These are paid features. However, all the core functionality of the website — including product listings, shopping cart, categories, and checkout — was successfully built and demonstrated.

## 4. Steps We Followed to Build the Website

- **Planning — Deciding What to Build**
  We first discussed what kind of website we wanted to make. We decided to build an online book store focused on books that are popular and useful in Pakistan — Urdu literature, Islamic books, and exam preparation guides. We divided the roles among ourselves and made a simple plan.

- **Creating a Wix Account**
  Muhammad Wasif Ul Zaman (Tech Lead) created a free Wix account and selected an e-commerce template that matched the style of a book store. The Wix Store app was added so we could list products and manage categories.

- **Designing the Website (Art Direction)**
  Omamah Naeem (Art Director) took charge of the visual design. She selected the background images, set the fonts, arranged the homepage layout, and wrote the welcome message. The tagline "Your Story Starts Here" was also finalized at this stage.

- **Creating Book Categories**
  We created three main product categories in Wix Store: Urdu Novels and Literature, Islamic Books, and Competitive Exams & Test Preparation. These categories appear in the navigation bar so customers can browse by type.

- **Adding Book Listings**
  Muhammad Ahmad Shah (Product Manager) added all 23 books to the store. For each book, we uploaded a cover image, set the title, assigned it to the correct category, and set both the original price and the sale/discounted price in Pakistani Rupees (PKR).

- **Setting Up Navigation**
  The navigation menu was configured with links to Shop All, Urdu Novels, Islamic Books, and Competitive Exam Books. The homepage buttons (Start Now, Shop All) were also linked to the correct pages. The About section was written and added.

- **Testing and Publishing**
  We tested all links, product pages, and the shopping cart before publishing. The website was previewed on both desktop and mobile to ensure it looked good on all screen sizes. Once satisfied, we published the website using Wix's free hosting.

## 7. Online Payment Error — Explanation & Screenshot

During testing of our website, when a customer adds books to the cart and proceeds to checkout, the following error message appears on the screen:

> **"We can't accept online payments"**

### 7.1 Why This Error Appears

This error is not a technical mistake or a bug in our website. It happens for one simple reason: we are using the free version of Wix, which does not support online payment processing. Here is a full explanation:

- **Free Wix Plan Restriction** — Wix only allows payment gateways (such as JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Stripe, or PayPal) on paid Business plans. The free plan cannot process real money transactions.
- **No Payment Provider Connected** — Even if we wanted to connect a payment method, that option is locked and greyed out in our Wix dashboard on the free plan.
- **Store Works in Demo Mode** — The Wix Store on the free plan is fully functional for display purposes. Products, categories, shopping cart, and the checkout page all work visually. However, the actual payment transaction cannot be completed.
- **Academic Project Purpose** — Since this website was built purely as a student project to learn how e-commerce websites are made, the free plan was sufficient for our goals.

This screenshot actually proves that our website is working correctly. The customer was able to browse products, add books to the cart, see correct prices in PKR, and reach the checkout page. The only limitation is the payment step, which requires a paid upgrade.

### 7.2 How to Fix This (Future Upgrade)

If this website were to be turned into a real business, we would upgrade to a **Wix Business Basic plan** and connect a Pakistani payment provider such as **JazzCash** or **EasyPaisa**, which would allow customers to complete their purchases online.
